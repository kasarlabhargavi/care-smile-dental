Tooth Extractions

Meet the Caring Hands Behind Your Smile

Meet Dr. Neelima Naidu

Dr. Neelima Naidu is a graduate of the UCSF School of Dentistry and has been practising dentistry in San Jose for over 20 years. She provides comprehensive, compassionate dental care, combining clinical experience with modern techniques to help patients maintain healthy, confident smiles.

With a gentle, patient-centred approach, Dr. Naidu cares for patients of all ages and backgrounds. She is a member of the California Dental Association (CDA), American Dental Association (ADA) and Santa Clara County Dental Society, reflecting her active involvement in the dental community.

Beyond the clinic, Dr. Naidu volunteers at local schools to educate children about dental health. She lives in San Jose with her husband and two children and enjoys cooking, painting and reading in her leisure time.
